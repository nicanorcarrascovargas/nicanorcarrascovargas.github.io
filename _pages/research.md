I am interested in theorems. Recently I have been working on entropy-type invariants and isomorphism questions for some skew products. In addition, part of my work is related to the open problem of classifying the Medvedev degrees (a notion of complexity) of subshifts of finite type on finitely generated groups. This program has taken me into questions in  effective graph theory and has led to new examples of groups without a generic Cantor system.
